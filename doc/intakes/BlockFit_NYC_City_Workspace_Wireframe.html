How to read these numbers

Newly covered = distinct requesters outside current equipment coverage who fall within a candidate’s radius. A + B counts their union, never their sum when coverage overlaps. Excluded sites contribute no coverage or costs. These are sample participants, not a population estimate.

Total cost = upfront cost + annual maintenance × planning years + replacement cost at each replacement interval strictly before the end of that period. Figures are undiscounted, in constant illustrative dollars, with no inflation or residual value. This screening comparison is not an official capital estimate, appraisal or financial ROI.

Cost ranges use editable low/high percentage assumptions. Missing cost inputs prevent saving; enter zero only when deliberately assumed. Land acquisition and site feasibility remain outside the model. Budget fit checks upfront spending only; recurring funding needs separate review.

Wireframe notes · implementation boundaries

  1. One primary decision: compare two candidate sites and their combined option for one equipment category. No citywide ranking or separate analytics dashboard.
  2. Map and table are equivalent entry points: map selection updates the detail card; every candidate remains accessible through a keyboard-friendly table button.
  3. Inventory drawer: demonstrate adding existing equipment independently or inside a named park. Coordinates here are schematic; the real MVP uses a map pin and geographic coordinates.
  4. Existing City work: public links and export support manual review. Recording demand or exporting a brief is not a City submission.
  5. Responsive layout: desktop has map and detail side by side; tablet/mobile stacks the same sections. The comparison table scrolls within its own container.
  6. Prototype boundary: changes last only for this tab session; reload resets them. No authentication, database, live basemap or AI service is implemented in this wireframe.
